## 1. Introduction

Pace Amigo ("we", "our", or "the App") is an ambient interval and focus timer designed with privacy by default. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it.

This Privacy Policy explains how Pace Amigo handles user information, device permissions, and data storage. **Pace Amigo does not collect, sell, or rent your personal information.**

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### A. Personal Data
- **We do not collect personal information.** We do not ask for, collect, or store your name, email address, phone number, physical address, financial information, or government identifiers.
- No user account or registration is required to use Pace Amigo.

### B. Usage & Timer Data
- **Timer Presets & History:** Your custom timer configurations, intervals, and session completion logs (e.g., duration, preset name, completion timestamp) are created solely to provide the timer functionality.
- **Local Storage:** All timer configurations, preferences, and session logs are stored **locally on your device** using local database storage (Hive). This data never leaves your device unless optional cloud sync is explicitly enabled.
- **Identifiers:** History logs utilize random, anonymous UUIDs generated locally on your device for database indexing. They cannot be used to personally identify you.

### C. Analytics and Advertising
- Pace Amigo **does not** contain third-party advertising SDKs (such as Google AdMob).
- Pace Amigo **does not** use behavioral tracking, third-party analytics trackers, or user profiling SDKs.

---

## 3. Device Permissions and How They Are Used

Pace Amigo requests only the minimal set of permissions strictly necessary to deliver core timer functionality:

| Permission | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- |
| **`POST_NOTIFICATIONS`** | Required on Android 13+ to send timer completion alerts and interval transition notifications when the app is in the background. |
| **`VIBRATE`** | Triggers tactile haptic feedback when intervals change or timers finish. |
| **`WAKE_LOCK`** | Keeps the screen awake during active timer sessions when ambient display mode is enabled. |
| **`INTERNET`** | Used to load typography (Google Fonts) and support optional cloud database synchronization if configured. |

You can review or revoke permissions at any time through your device's **Settings > Apps > Pace Amigo > Permissions**.

---

## 4. Third-Party Services

The App may interact with the following third-party services:

- **Google Fonts:** The app uses Google Fonts to render typography. Requests to Google Fonts may include your IP address as part of standard HTTP requests, processed in accordance with the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- **Google Play Services:** The app is distributed through Google Play, which may collect diagnostic information and installation metrics in accordance with Google Play's standard terms.

---

## 5. Data Retention and Deletion

- Because your data is stored locally on your device, you have complete control over it.
- **Deleting Data:** You can delete your session history at any time from within the app settings, or clear all app data via **Android Settings > Apps > Pace Amigo > Storage > Clear Data**.
- **Uninstalling:** Uninstalling Pace Amigo from your device completely and permanently deletes all locally stored data.

## 6. Children’s Privacy

Pace Amigo does not address anyone under the age of 13 (or the minimum legal age in your jurisdiction), nor does it knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so that we can take necessary action.

## 7. Security

We prioritize the security of your data. Because your data is kept locally on your own device and we do not operate centralized user databases of personal data, the risk of data interception or unauthorized access through external servers is minimized.
