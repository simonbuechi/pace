Privacy Policy for Pace Amigo

Last updated: September 12, 2026
Application Name: Pace Amigo
Application ID: ch.simibu.pace
Developer: Simon Büchi
Key Takeaway: Pace Amigo is an ambient interval timer built with privacy by default. We do not collect, sell, or rent your personal information. All your timers, intervals, and history stay locally on your device.

1. Introduction

Pace Amigo ("we", "our", or "the App") is an ambient interval and focus timer designed to respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how Pace Amigo handles user information, device permissions, and data storage.

2. Information We Collect

A. Personal Data

We do not collect personal information. We do not ask for, collect, or store your name, email address, phone number, physical address, financial information, or government identifiers. No user account or registration is required to use the App.

B. Usage & Timer Data

  • Timer Presets & History: Your custom interval configurations and completion logs (duration, intervals, timestamp) are processed strictly to deliver timer features.
  • Local Storage: All session data and settings are stored locally on your device using local database storage (Hive).
  • Identifiers: Session records use locally generated anonymous UUIDs solely for indexing. They cannot be used to personally identify you.

C. Analytics and Advertising

  • Pace Amigo does not contain third-party advertising SDKs.
  • Pace Amigo does not use behavioral tracking or third-party analytics trackers.

3. Device Permissions

Pace Amigo requests only the minimal permissions required for core timer functionality:

Permission Purpose
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Sends timer transition and completion alerts when the app is running in the background.
VIBRATE Provides tactile haptic countdown feedback for intervals and transitions.
WAKE_LOCK Keeps the screen awake during active focus sessions when ambient display is enabled.
INTERNET Loads typography (Google Fonts) and supports optional cloud backup if configured.

4. Third-Party Services

The App may interact with the following third-party services:

  • Google Fonts: The app renders typography using Google Fonts. Fonts are fetched via standard HTTP requests subject to the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play Services: Distributed via Google Play, which may collect standard platform diagnostics and install stats in accordance with Google Play terms.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Because your data is stored locally on your device, you have complete control over it:

  • Deleting History: You can clear your session history at any time from within the app settings or via Android Settings > Apps > Pace Amigo > Storage > Clear Data.
  • Uninstalling: Uninstalling the app permanently and immediately removes all local data from your device.

6. Children’s Privacy

Pace Amigo does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or the minimum legal age in your jurisdiction).

7. Security

Your session data remains on your device. Because we do not operate centralized user databases of personal data, the risk of data compromise or external interception is minimized.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. The revised policy will be posted here with an updated "Last updated" date.
